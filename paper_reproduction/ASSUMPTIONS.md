* **D** — directly specified by the paper
* **d** — derived from a paper equation (no freedom)
* **I** — inferred from the paper's figures/text (freedom removed by evidence)
* **N** — standard numerical assumption
* **U** — unknown / unspecified, choice documented and its effect quantified

## Deliberate non-assumptions

The following were **not** done, on purpose:

* No parameter was tuned to make a figure look like the paper's. Table I is used verbatim.
  The only equation deviation is the sign in A1, which is justified by evidence, is a one-line
  switch, and whose literal alternative is shown to fail.
* The paper's Figs. 5–6 translation rate is **not** matched by adjusting `c`, `u^n`, `m` or the
  time axis. `results/data/progress_diagnostic.mat` reports what single-parameter change *would*
  reconcile it (`c ≈ 1.25` or `‖u^n‖ ≈ 3.6`), purely as a diagnostic.
* No result is hard-coded. Every number in `VALIDATION.md` is produced by
  `validate_results.m` from the simulation output at run time.
